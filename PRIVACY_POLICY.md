# Deejay Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deejay Bot ("Deejay," the "Bot," "we," "us,"
or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Bot
on Discord.

## 1. Information We Process

### Information received from Discord

When you interact with Deejay, we may process:

- your Discord user ID;
- the Discord server and channel IDs involved in a command;
- voice-channel membership needed to connect the Bot and authorize controls;
- slash-command options, button interactions, and playlist names you submit;
  and
- technical interaction metadata required to respond through Discord.

Deejay does not intentionally read ordinary chat messages, record voice audio,
or collect your Discord password.

### Music and playlist information

We process song searches and music metadata, which may include track titles,
artists, artwork URLs, source identifiers, durations, and YouTube URLs. Saved
playlists associate this information with your Discord user ID and server ID.

### Technical information

The service may create operational logs containing timestamps, errors,
Lavalink connection events, and limited technical identifiers needed to
diagnose failures and protect the service. We do not use the Bot for behavioral
advertising.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- execute music searches and play requested tracks;
- manage per-server queues and voice connections;
- save, display, edit, and delete user playlists;
- restrict playback controls to the appropriate voice channel;
- maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

## 3. Legal Bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as needed
to provide the service you request, based on our legitimate interests in
operating and securing Deejay, with your consent where required, or to comply
with legal obligations.

## 4. Storage and Retention

Active playback queues and voice state are held in memory and are normally
discarded when playback ends, the Bot disconnects, or the service restarts.

Saved playlist data is stored in a SQLite database and remains until you delete
the playlist or request deletion. Removing Deejay from a Discord server does
not currently trigger automatic deletion. Operational logs are retained only
as long as reasonably necessary for security and troubleshooting.

We may retain information longer when required by law, needed to resolve a
dispute, or necessary to protect the service.

## 5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information. We share or transmit limited information
only as needed to operate the Bot:

- **Discord** delivers interactions, user and server identifiers, and voice
  connection events.
- **YouTube and Lavalink providers** may receive music searches, track
  identifiers, or URLs to supply metadata and resolve playable audio.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** may process database records and
  logs on our behalf.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.
Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or
safety, or as part of a reorganization or transfer of the service.

## 6. International Transfers

Discord and other service providers may process information in countries other
than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for
international transfers.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect
stored information, including limiting requested Discord permissions and
keeping credentials outside the source repository. No system is completely
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

You can delete playlists with the Bot's `/playlist delete` command and can stop
further collection by no longer using the Bot. Depending on your location, you
may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to
processing of your personal information, or to receive a portable copy.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the address below and include
your Discord user ID and relevant server ID. We may need to verify your
identity before completing the request.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Deejay is not intended for children under 13 or under the minimum age required
to use Discord in their country. We do not knowingly collect information from
children who are not permitted to use Discord. Contact us if you believe such
information has been provided.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the Bot, its providers, or legal requirements
change. The "Last updated" date will show when revisions were made. Material
changes may also be announced through an appropriate Discord channel or the
Bot's public documentation.
